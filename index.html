📖 Technical Specifications & Guidelines

Section 1: Technical Definition of standard Pip & Pipette

In the foreign exchange market, currency movements are measured using Pips (Percentage in Point). A pip represents the standard unit of price change. To enhance precision, modern financial brokerage servers track an additional decimal fractional element called a Pipette.

Standard Equivalency: 1 Pip = 10 Pipettes

Note: Pipette is the sub-unit. For example, in EUR/USD, a price difference from 1.08500 to 1.08510 equals a 1 Pip (10 Pipettes) movement.

Bilingual Operational Context:

  • Forex market me price ki movement ko measure karne ke liye Standard Pip use kiya jata hai.
  • Yahi currency exchange rate me hone wala sabse chota standard price move hai.
  • Brokers accuracy badhane ke liye standard pips ke baad ek aur fractional digit display karte hain, jise Pipette kehte hain.

Section 2: Pair-Specific Pip Counting Methodology

Because currency quotes differ structurally, they are divided into three standard categories to compute gains cleanly:

Category A: 4-Decimal / 5-Decimal Pairs (Majors & Minors) Target: EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, USD/CAD, NZD/USD

The 4th decimal is the main pip. For example, BUY EUR/USD Entry 1.08500, Exit 1.09120 = +62.0 Pips.

Category B: Japanese Yen (JPY) Pairs (2-Decimal / 3-Decimal) Target: USD/JPY, EUR/JPY, GBP/JPY, AUD/JPY

The Yen valuation uses the 2nd decimal for the standard pip. For example, BUY USD/JPY Entry 155.200, Exit 156.450 = +125.0 Pips.

Category C: Commodities & Metals (Gold - XAU/USD) Target: Gold Spot

Gold tracks standard pips at the 1st decimal (every $0.10 price difference equals 1 Pip). A full $1.00 move equals 10 Pips. For example, BUY entry $2350.00, Exit $2365.50 = +155.0 Pips.

Section 3: Lot Size Architecture & Per-Pip Values

Lot size describes transaction quantity. Pip dollar values are standard for accounts backed by USD as the capital asset:

Designation Volume Value Base Equivalence Per-Pip Value (USD)
Standard Lot 1.00 100,000 units $10.00
Mini Lot 0.10 10,000 units $1.00
Micro Lot 0.01 1,000 units $0.10

Universal Valuation Formula:

Profit/Loss = Total Pips * Lot Size * Standard Pip Value ($10.00)

Section 4: Professional Risk Management Framework

Discipline separates quant models from gamblers. Follow the space-age rule parameters to preserve equity:

🛡️ The 1% to 2% Risk Cap

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your total capital balance on a single trade. If your account size is $10,000, your Stop Loss boundary should strictly enforce a maximum loss parameter of $100 to $200!

📈 Fixed Risk-Reward (RR) Execution

Always enter market positions with structured Take Profit (TP) and Stop Loss (SL) triggers. Ensure your strategy metrics present a minimum of 1:2 Risk-to-Reward (Reward should be at least double the risk).
